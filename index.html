About the creator

A Reverse Murder Mystery

Three people from different parts of the city have died. The police think the deaths may be connected, but the victims seem to have nothing in common.

But you know the truth, the deaths are connected, and you are the person behind the three identities the police are investigating. Your goal is to learn what the investigators know without letting them connect the murders and identities to you.

Three identities. Three investigations.

Places You Can Visit

  • Your apartment
  • The police station
  • A hotel
  • A business office
  • The first victim's apartment
  • A university

Your choices affect how much you learn, how suspicious the investigators become, and whether your identities are connected.
